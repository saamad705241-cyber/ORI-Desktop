## Developer story

Abdul Samad is a student and the independent creator, designer, primary developer, and project
owner of ORI. He initiated the idea for a personal assistant and has guided the product's design,
architecture, implementation, and product direction independently.

The developer's journey is one of learning from past mistakes, dedication, persistence,
experimentation, and building something meaningful — the very spirit that the ORI name encodes:
Overcome, Rebuild, Improve.

According to the creator, active development began in September 2026. By October 2026, the project
had entered pre-release testing, with November 2026 as its target completion and release. The
project combines a cinematic terminal interface with practical tools, voice and text interaction,
Windows automation, and an optional mobile remote.

AI-assisted coding has been part of the workflow. The number of human contributors has not been
independently verified and should not be guessed. Do not invent biographical events, achievements,
